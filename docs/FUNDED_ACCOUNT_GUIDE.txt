============================================================
QUANTA — FUNDED ACCOUNT GUIDE
============================================================
For Indian traders targeting FTMO, E8 Funding, or The5ers

============================================================
WHAT IS A FUNDED ACCOUNT?
============================================================

A prop firm (proprietary trading firm) gives you a larger account
to trade with, in exchange for passing their evaluation and sharing
a portion of profits.

You pay a challenge fee (₹8,000 – ₹12,000).
You trade their demo account following their rules.
If you pass → they give you real funded capital ($10K – $200K).
You keep 70-90% of profits.
If you lose too much → you fail and lose the challenge fee.

WHY this system is built this way:
  Every risk rule in Quanta is designed to keep you within prop
  firm limits so you never accidentally fail a challenge.

============================================================
WHICH FIRM TO START WITH?
============================================================

Recommendation for beginners: The5ers Bootcamp ($6,000 account)
  Why: No time limit, instant funding option, cheaper fee
  Fee: ~$80 = ~₹6,500
  Rules: Max daily loss 4%, max total loss 6%
  Profit target: 8%
  Payout: weekly, via Wise

Alternative: E8 Funding ($10K account)
  Fee: ~$120 = ~₹10,000
  Rules: Max daily loss 5%, max total loss 8%
  Profit target: 8%
  Payout: bi-weekly

Advanced (after 1 successful cycle): FTMO ($10K account)
  Fee: ~$155 = ~₹12,800
  Rules: Max daily loss 5%, max total loss 10%
  Profit target: 10% (phase 1), 5% (phase 2)
  Payout: monthly

============================================================
BEFORE STARTING A CHALLENGE
============================================================

Required checks (ALL must pass):

□ Backtest: 200+ trades, PF ≥ 1.2, Sharpe ≥ 0.8
□ Demo trading: 50+ trades, no DD breach
□ Kill switch tested from phone (Telegram /kill → /reset)
□ News filter tested (block zone working)
□ Lot size calculator verified (check on demo)
□ Daily DD counter resets at midnight correctly
□ All systems show GREEN on dashboard
□ MT5 on VPS is stable (uptime >7 days)
□ Compliance engine loaded with correct firm rules

============================================================
DURING THE CHALLENGE
============================================================

Daily monitoring:
  Morning: Check last night's closes (Asian session)
  12:30 IST: Be alert for London open signals
  After each trade: Check execution quality
  Evening: Review daily PnL and DD remaining

Warning zones (step back and review):
  Daily loss > 2.5% (for 4% max limit)
  Total loss > 4% (for 6% max limit)

Hard stops (system auto-activates kill switch):
  Daily loss > 3% (buffer before 4% firm limit)
  Total loss > 5% (buffer before 6% firm limit)

If you reach the warning zone:
  → Do NOT take any more trades that day
  → Review what went wrong
  → Only resume when you have a clear head and fresh day

============================================================
AFTER PASSING: LIVE FUNDED ACCOUNT
============================================================

Withdrawal process:
  1. Meet minimum withdrawal threshold (usually $500)
  2. Request withdrawal through firm's portal
  3. They send via bank wire or Wise/PayPal
  4. Use Wise (wise.com) for best USD → INR conversion

Tax implications (India):
  Income from prop trading = Business income under ITR-3
  Keep all records:
    - Challenge fee paid (deductible as business expense)
    - VPS costs (deductible)
    - All trade records (Quanta auto-logs these)
    - All withdrawal records
  File under Schedule BP (Business Profession income)
  Applicable GST: 18% if annual income > ₹20 lakh

IMPORTANT: Consult a CA for your specific tax situation.
This is general guidance, not tax advice.

Month 3-4:  Scale to $25K (add more capital from the firm)
Month 5-6:  Scale to $50K or open a second account
Year 1:     Target ₹1,50,000 – ₹3,00,000/month profit

Scaling rules:
  NEVER increase lot size during the challenge phase
  NEVER override the risk rules
  ALWAYS keep 2+ months of VPS costs in savings as buffer
  NEVER use rent/emergency money for challenge fees

============================================================
IF YOU FAIL A CHALLENGE
============================================================

It happens. Even to professionals. Here is what to do:

1. Download all trade history from the firm's portal
2. Import into Quanta backtester for post-mortem analysis
3. Identify: Why did the system fail the rules?
   a. Did a news event cause a large loss?
   b. Was the strategy in a losing streak?
   c. Was risk per trade too high?
4. Run walk-forward analysis again with the updated data
5. Paper trade for 2 more weeks before re-challenging
6. Consider starting with a smaller account size to reduce fee

Typical failure reasons:
  - Impatience: trading during low quality setups to "make it back"
  - Overriding the system during losing streaks
  - News event slippage causing unexpected losses
  - System not being monitored → crash undetected for hours

Quanta is built to prevent these — but only if you follow the rules.
