Behavior Contract

1) Triggering:
- Regime computation runs only on CANDLE_CLOSED event.
- Non-H1 candles are ignored by detector runtime path.

2) Safety:
- No fake regime is forced when data is insufficient.
- Waiting states are explicitly published to Redis/UI.
- Validator rejects unstable low-confidence/persistence states.

3) Outputs:
- Coarse regime enum (backward compatibility)
- 12-state regime label
- Probabilities per 12-state regime
- Transition state
- Strategy mapping guidance (enable/disable + risk mode)

4) Performance:
- Event-driven, no polling loops.
- Dashboard analytics use cached queries.
